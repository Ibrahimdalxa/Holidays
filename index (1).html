Special Alert: May Day + Friday

Dhaka City Access Guide

Today is Friday, May 1, 2026. Because it is International Workers' Day (May Day)—a gazetted government holiday—combined with the standard Friday weekend, operating hours for tourist destinations in Dhaka are severely impacted. Most government-run historical sites and museums are closed, while open-air parks and private venues generally remain open.

Strategic Advice for Today

🛑

Avoid Government Heritage Sites

Locations like Lalbagh Fort, Ahsan Manzil, and the National Museum adhere strictly to public holiday closures.

🌳

Embrace Open Spaces

Public parks (Suhrawardy Udyan, Ramna Park) and large outdoor areas (Hatirjheel) are your best bets. Expect larger crowds than usual.

Parks & Zoo are Open

The National Zoo and Botanical Garden typically remain open on public holidays to accommodate the high volume of local visitors.
